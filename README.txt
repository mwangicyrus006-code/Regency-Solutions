HOW THE FORMS WORK (FormSubmit, free, no account)
Every form (pricing plan, Start a Project, contact query, newsletter) sends an email to solutionsregency@gmail.com,
and the visitor automatically gets a confirmation. Pricing requests auto-reply with your pricing plan link.

ONE-TIME ACTIVATION
1. Put the site online (or open it from a web address, not by double-clicking the file).
2. Submit any form once. FormSubmit emails solutionsregency@gmail.com an activation message. Click the confirm button in it.
3. Submit again to test. You should get the email, and the sender gets the auto-reply.
(Check Spam/Promotions the first time.)

SETTINGS: open script.js and edit the block at the top:
- PRICING_LINK: link to your pricing plan PDF (upload to Google Drive and share "anyone with link", or put the PDF in the site folder)
- SOCIAL: paste your Facebook / X / LinkedIn / Instagram page links; icons then open them in a new tab

PRICING PLAN: the link is a Google Sheet. In Google Sheets click Share > General access > "Anyone with the link" > role VIEWER (never Editor), otherwise visitors will see "Request access".
WHATSAPP / SOCIAL: edit the CFG block at the top of script.js.
